Sources: Weather, water temp, waves & ocean current from Open-Meteo · Tides from NOAA CO-OPS (US) or modeled sea level · Chlorophyll-a and sea-temperature grids from NOAA CoastWatch (VIIRS / blended-analysis satellite feeds, newest fresh source used and named) · Moon phase computed locally. Waves, water, current, tide & chlorophyll are marine/satellite data and cover coastal & ocean areas (satellite readings can have gaps under cloud cover). The bite score is a heuristic guide based on pressure, wind, light, tide movement, sea state, water productivity and moon — not a guarantee. Always check official marine forecasts before heading out.
